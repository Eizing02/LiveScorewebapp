Football-Data.co.uk and its derived mirrors are not used: the source explicitly restricts automated bots/scrapers/AI data-training products. Other reviewed sources not admitted are listed in reports/historical-source-catalog.json. Public access alone is not permission to republish or train.

The historical-source notices above do not assign a license to API-Football material. Authenticated responses and player-availability records used by the separate private research collector remain in ignored local storage; credentials are excluded from application source and exports. Admission to a private research ledger is not automatic publication of that ledger.

UI branding assets are cached separately from the historical result database. Team/league PNGs were retrieved by provider identity from the API-Sports media CDN; exact source URLs, byte lengths and SHA-256 values are recorded in `apps/web/public/media/sources.json`. IBM Plex Sans Thai is self-hosted for the interface, with its accompanying SIL Open Font License in `apps/web/public/fonts/OFL.txt`.

The one-page programme retains the same 47 static PNG image payloads and embeds their source origins as metadata. The manifest preserves original downloaded hashes/lengths as `download_sha256` / `download_bytes` and records current metadata-bearing file hashes/lengths as `sha256` / `bytes`. Additional known catalogue IDs may be read from the fixed API-Sports image CDN through the private cached media route; it sends no API-Football account key and does not grant broader publication rights.

Precision v3 reuses the same adapted recent-v2 ledger and source provenance; no
Hudl/StatsBomb event data is included. Its license was inspected separately and
was not treated as permission to train or republish this application. Private
prospective OpenLigaDB research records retain ODbL attribution and unverified
mapping/status markers, and are not part of the accepted training dataset.
